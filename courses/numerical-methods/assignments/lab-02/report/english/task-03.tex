
\section{Task 3}
We test an algorithm that computes the inverse of an upper-triangular matrix.\\
\textbf{Operation of invU}
\begin{itemize}
    \item loop over columns
    \item loop over rows
    \item accumulate products of successive elements
\end{itemize}

\begin{center}
\resizebox{0.27\textwidth}{!}{
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|}
\hline
$n$    & invU       \\ \hline
100  & $0.906316$s \\ \hline
1000 & $1.43726$s \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
}
\end{center}
Inverting upper-triangular matrices is very fast.

\section{Task 1}
We compare different algorithms for matrix multiplication.
\textbf{Operation of mm}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item loop over the row indices of matrix A
    \item loop over the column indices of matrix B
    \item loop over the column indices of matrix A
    \item accumulate each element by multiplying the corresponding row and column entries
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Operation of mm1}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item loop over the row indices of matrix A
    \item loop over the column indices of matrix B
    \item multiply the corresponding rows of A and columns of B
\end{enumerate}
